  <h1>Privacy Policy — Still Here</h1>
  <p><strong>Last updated:</strong> April 6, 2026</p>
  <p>Still Here is a daily check-in app. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.</p>

  <h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Check-in data:</strong> timestamps, streak count — stored locally on your device.</li>
    <li><strong>Display name:</strong> a callsign you choose.</li>
    <li><strong>Camera:</strong> used only to scan QR codes. No photos stored.</li>
    <li><strong>Notifications:</strong> local only, not sent to our servers.</li>
    <li><strong>Telegram Chat ID:</strong> only if you enable Deadman Switch, stored locally.</li>
    <li><strong>Friends/Crew data:</strong> user ID, display name, check-in status stored in our database.</li>
    <li><strong>Purchase data:</strong> managed by RevenueCat. We do not store payment details.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>2. How We Use Your Information</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>To display your streak, stats, and countdown</li>
    <li>To schedule local notifications</li>
    <li>To show your status to friends</li>
    <li>To send your Deadman Switch message if you miss a check-in</li>
    <li>To verify your subscription status</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>3. Data Sharing</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Supabase</strong> — database for social features</li>
    <li><strong>Firebase</strong> — real-time social features</li>
    <li><strong>RevenueCat</strong> — subscription management</li>
    <li><strong>Telegram</strong> — only if Deadman Switch is enabled</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>4. Data Retention</h2>
  <p>Local data stays on your device until you uninstall. Social data is retained until you request deletion.</p>

  <h2>5. Your Rights</h2>
  <p>You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.</p>

  <h2>6. Children's Privacy</h2>
  <p>This app is not intended for users under 13 years of age.</p>
